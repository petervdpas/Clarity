## The board

Nine squares in three rows. You play the **crosses** and always move first; the computer plays the **noughts**.

## Playing

1. Click an empty square to place your cross.
2. The computer answers at once with a nought.

## Winning

- The first with **three in a row** wins: across, down or diagonal.
- When all nine squares are full and no one has three in a row, it is a *draw*.

## Tip

The computer completes its own row when it can, blocks yours when you are about to win, and likes the centre square. Plan **two threats at once** to beat it.

## Keys

- `Ctrl+N` starts a new game.
- *Game > Another window* opens a second board.
