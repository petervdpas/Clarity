## Prologue: the lamp



## Arrival

The boat left her on the jetty an hour before the storm closed the sound.

She climbed the steps with the wind at her back, counting them the way the letter had told her to: one hundred and twelve, and a door that would not be locked.

It was not. Inside, the lamp room smelled of oil and salt, and on the table, under a stone, lay a book bound in sailcloth.
